Phase 01 • Diagnostic Intake

Every flaw tells a story.

Before machine polishes touch clear coat, our studio conducts a surgical 30-point intake audit. Paint depth gauges measure lacquer in microns (120–140µm baseline) to detect transport marring, micro-scratches, and high-velocity road fallout from Chennai highways.

Clear Coat Depth Scan 30-Point Surface Audit OMR • Kottivakkam Studio Paint Gauge Inspection
Phase 02 • Chemical Unmasking

Purity before performance.

Component separation exposes deep contaminants. High-density pH-neutral snow foam lifts grit without friction, while acid-free wheel cleaners dissolve metallic brake dust from calipers and inner wheel barrels. Stripped back to virgin factory clear coat.

pH-Neutral Snow Foam Iron Chemical Dissolution Wheel Barrel & Caliper Clean Underbody Pressure Flush
Phase 03 • Paint Correction

Clear coat is finite. Treat it like diamond.

Multi-stage dual-action machine compounding levels microscopic clear-coat hills and valleys. We safely remove 2 to 3 microns of oxidized lacquer to eradicate swirl marks, bird-dropping etching, and holograms—recovering optical depth without compromising panel thickness.

Dual-Action Rotary Surgery Swirl & Hologram Eradication Zero-Filler Compounding True 94+ GU Specular Depth
Phase 04 • Ceramic 9H Nanomatrix

Liquid glass, hardened for the road.

True 9H ceramic nanomatrix chemically bonds at the sub-molecular lattice, creating an impermeable hydrophobic barrier (>115° water contact angle). Engineered specifically to shield against Chennai's corrosive coastal salinity, acid rain, and brutal 40°C tropical UV.

9H Sub-Molecular Matrix >115° Hydrophobic Angle Coastal Salt & UV Shield Candy Hard-Gloss Finish
Phase 05 • Aliphatic TPU PPF

Self-healing defense against the open road.

Optical-grade 8mil aliphatic thermoplastic polyurethane (TPU) computer-cut to factory panel dimensions. Features an elastomeric self-healing topcoat that instantly erases swirl marks under sunlight, absorbing gravel impact, stone chips, and high-speed highway debris.

8mil Optical Aliphatic TPU Instant Heat Self-Healing Wrapped Edges & Zero Gaps High-Impact Stone Chip Shield
Phase 06 • Cockpit & Mechanical Care

Every surface. Considered.

Total mechanical and cockpit devotion. Delicate aniline leather is cleansed and nourished with matte pH-balanced balms; cockpit screens receive anti-static oleophobic protection; high-temperature ceramic shields insulate brake calipers and the rear powertrain.

Aniline Leather Hydration Anti-Static Matte Cockpit High-Temp Engine Ceramic Crystal Rain Glass Repellent
Phase 07 • Showroom Handover

Not just clean. Carzzap perfected.

As the components re-unify into a flawless silhouette, the vehicle undergoes final inspection under 5000K daylight simulation arrays. The result is pure optical symmetry—a mirror finish calibrated for Chennai's streets with certified gloss readings exceeding 99.8 GU.
